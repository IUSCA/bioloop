What changed from the page these screens replace, and why.

Departures from the current page

Page width
The page was max-w-4xl, which left most of a wide viewport empty. It is max-w-7xl like every other v2 page, in two columns.
Page title
An h1 reading "Access request" repeated the breadcrumb. The heading names the resource instead, which is the thing the reader came for.
Key/value rows
Labels were left and values hard right, with the width of the card between them. Rows are left-aligned against a fixed label column.
Accent surfaces
Resource, subject, and purpose each had a different tinted box. Tint now means status, and nothing else.
Access type naming
Rows led with DATASET:LIST_FILES. They lead with "Browse file tree" and keep the identifier as monospace secondary text, matching the grant rows and the selector.
Status
The status badge appeared twice. It appears once, beside the heading.
Card titles
Sentence-case text-lg headings became the uppercase letterspaced card title the other v2 surfaces use.
Outcome
Live, revoked, and expired counts were inline badges. They are a stat row at the top of the rail, because on a decided request they are the first question.
Coverage
"Also reaching this subject" was a paragraph list at the bottom of the summary card. It is its own card, and it says what the reader should do with it.

Rules these screens follow

Data
Every field shown is already on the request response. Nothing here needs a new endpoint or a schema change.
Colour
Green means in force, amber means changed since the decision, red means gone or refused, blue means informational. Neutral for everything else.
Never colour alone
Every tone carries a word. A revoked outcome says so in a sentence, not only in red.
Cards that earn a place
The outcome card is absent on a rejected request, and the coverage card is absent when nothing else reaches the subject. An empty card is worse than no card.
Layers
Vuestic supplies the buttons, badges, cards, and modal. Tailwind supplies the grid and the one-off surface. No new primitive is needed.
