# Groups Domain Glossary

These terms come from academic research computing and do not always mean what
the same words mean elsewhere in software. This page fixes their meaning for the
[groups design](./design.md), the [use cases](./use-cases.md), and the code that
implements them.

## Organizational entities

**Lab** — A research group led by a PI (Principal Investigator). The primary
organizational unit. Data products are typically generated here, by processing
source or raw data produced at a core. Labs may collaborate with other labs or
sit inside a larger center.

**Group** — The system's structural abstraction for *any* organizational unit:
labs, projects, centers, cores. Modelled once so that membership, permissions,
and hierarchy work uniformly regardless of what the group represents in the real
world.

**Project** — A type of group focused on a specific research effort or data
collection activity. Contains datasets (source, raw, and derived data products)
and may be linked to a grant, a lab, or a core facility. Treated like any other
group structurally, but carries extra metadata such as project goals.

**Grant / Program Grant** — A funding source or umbrella initiative. May contain
sub-grants or projects and is often associated with multiple labs or cores. Used
to track which datasets were produced with which funding.

**Core** — A shared resource or facility, such as a genomics, imaging, or
bioinformatics core. Projects consume services from one or more cores, and
source and raw data is generated at cores.

**Dataset** — A collection of research data generated by a project or lab. Has
metadata (method, date, contributors) and can be associated with more than one
project, lab, or grant.

## Access control terms

**Permission** — A rule determining which users may view, edit, or manage a
group, project, or dataset.

**Cascade** — Whether permissions held on a parent group (for example, a center)
apply to its subgroups and projects.

**Discoverability** — Whether a group or project is visible to users who are not
members, in the manner of a public profile or directory listing.

## Roles

**User** — A researcher or analyst with access to one or more groups.

**PI (Principal Investigator)** — Typically owns a lab or a grant, and can manage
users and data beneath those groups.

**Group Owner** — Approves membership requests and manages permissions within a
group.

**Platform Admin** — Holds elevated permissions to configure the structure and
policy of the platform itself. Distinct from a group admin, whose authority is
scoped to their group.
